Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2023

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy describes how CONSTRUCTION OS ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our industrial field operations platform. We are committed to protecting your professional data and ensuring transparency in our processing operations.

2. DATA COLLECTION

We collect information directly from you and automatically through your use of the platform, including but not limited to:

  • Geolocation data for site safety verification
  • Biometric data for high-security access zones
  • Equipment telemetry linked to operator profiles
  • Operational logs and safety checklists

3. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

The primary purpose of data processing is the optimization of job site safety and operational efficiency. Your data helps us predict maintenance needs and ensure compliance with federal OSHA standards.
