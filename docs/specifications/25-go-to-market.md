## 25.1 Entry Strategy

Start with :

> Mid-sized contractors

because :

- Excel pain is severe
- ERP adoption is low
- Decision cycle faster than enterprise

Definition — Mid-sized Contractor :

| Dimension | Target Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Annual construction volume | 50 million – 2 billion THB |
| Concurrent active projects | 3 – 20 projects |
| Headcount | 20 – 500 employees |
| Current tooling | Excel, LINE, basic accounting software (Express, AccPac, or equivalent) |
| Decision cycle | 2–6 weeks (owner or COO decision, no multi-month procurement committee) |

This segment is the primary target because :

- Large enough to have measurable pain (cost overruns, reporting delays, procurement leakage)
- Small enough that the owner can decide without multi-month procurement committee cycles
- Under-served by international ERP (Procore, Oracle) due to price, complexity, and language barrier

Enterprise (2 billion+ THB annual volume, 500+ headcount) is a secondary target reached
via expansion from existing mid-market contracts, not cold sales. See 29-final-strategic-positioning
section 29.4 for the full market opportunity analysis.
